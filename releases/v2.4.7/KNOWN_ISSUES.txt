QuietShield 2.4.7 Code 272 Final R1 - Known Issues
======================================================

The application build is released as final Code 272, while Video Settings and
video functions remain Beta.

Known website/device-dependent limitations:

- Picture-in-Picture may still show a black video surface on some websites,
  protected media, proprietary players, WebView versions, or device firmware.
- YouTube's fullscreen settings cogwheel may remain unavailable on some player
  layouts even when nearby controls such as CC are clickable.
- Website seeking may be unavailable when the player does not expose a usable
  HTML5 media interface; QuietShield now fails silently in that case.
- Brightness and volume gestures may vary by website touch layout and can be
  restricted to avoid intercepting native player controls.
- Fullscreen surface exit/restoration remains dependent on the website player
  and Android WebView implementation.

Core VPN protection, licensing, permanent signer continuity, App Connection
Lock policies, and verified Direct GitHub update delivery remain release gates.
