# QuietShield 2.4.7 — Final Code 272 R1

Stable Direct GitHub application release for Android phones and tablets.

## Private Browser

- Supports up to two tabs.
- Pauses media in the inactive tab to reduce unnecessary RAM, battery, and bandwidth use.
- A confirmed **Close Privacy Browser** action closes the private session and opens Home/Speed Dials on the next launch.
- Unexpected process closure can restore the previous private session.
- Closing the final tab resets its label and view to **🏠 Home**.

## Fullscreen and video

- QuietShield Lock and PiP controls fade after five seconds and return after a screen touch.
- Left-side vertical gestures adjust temporary window brightness; right-side gestures adjust media volume.
- Active video can keep the screen awake, with the flag released after playback or fullscreen ends.
- Fullscreen exit and recovery safeguards are retained.
- Unsupported player seeking is handled silently rather than displaying an obstruction over the video.

### Video Beta notice

**Video Settings and video functions remain Beta in this final app release.**

Website-specific behavior can still vary by Android WebView, device firmware, protected media, and player implementation. Picture-in-Picture may render black on some players; YouTube’s fullscreen settings cogwheel may remain unavailable on some layouts; seeking and brightness/volume gestures may not work consistently on every website.

## App Connection Lock

- Filters include All, Blocked, Wi-Fi Only, Allowed on mobile data, and Allowed on both.
- Block all user apps continues to exclude system apps by default.
- Advanced system apps can be shown and given manual connection rules only after a warning.
- QuietShield and essential Android components retain safeguards.
