QuietShield 2.4.6 Code 271 R3.3 - Known Issues
====================================================

This final Direct GitHub release is being published with acknowledged
remaining bugs.

- Some website-specific fullscreen controls may behave differently depending
  on the player layout and device.
- Picture-in-Picture rendering and restoration may still vary with WebView,
  protected media, proprietary players, or device firmware.
- Embedded website captions, settings controls, and gesture hit regions may
  need additional site-specific refinement.

Core VPN protection, licensing, permanent signer continuity, and verified
Direct GitHub update delivery remain release gates.
