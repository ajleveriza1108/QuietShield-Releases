QuietShield 2.5.1 Code 276 — Known Issues
================================================

1. Private Browser prolonged-video termination
A tester reported a random Private Browser termination after roughly ten
minutes of YouTube playback. No QuietShield application exception was captured,
so the exact cause is not proven. Code 276 adds WebView renderer recovery, but
OEM process termination, low-memory process death, WebView defects, and
website-specific failures cannot be distinguished from that single report.

2. Website session restoration
Renderer recovery reloads the affected tab's last known URL and does not
deliberately clear normal cookies. A website can still expire or reject its own
session, and in-page playback position may be lost after a renderer or process
restart.

3. YouTube fullscreen settings
The YouTube fullscreen settings cogwheel may remain unresponsive on some
layouts even while the adjacent CC button works.

4. Picture-in-Picture
Private Browser PiP remains removed because previous implementations produced a
black video surface on tested devices.

5. Smart Skip Beta
Smart Skip relies on community timestamps. A skip button may be absent,
incorrect, or delayed. No microphone recording or continuous speech analysis is
performed.

6. Fullscreen video Beta
Brightness, volume, seeking, Lock, and website controls remain dependent on
Android WebView, OEM firmware, and the website player's own touch behavior.
