QuietShield 2.5.5 (Code 280)
================================

Private Browser
---------------
- Preferred video quality defaults to 1080p. When a supported player does not expose 1080p, QuietShield selects 720p when available, then the best lower exposed level.
- Adds 1080p, 720p, and Automatic choices under Private Browser settings.
- Home tabs now show the QuietShield app icon and one plain Home label. The doubled house icon/title is removed.
- Restores the regular-browser Close Private Browser prompt when closing from the menu or pressing the phone Back action from Browser Home.
- Adds Clear browsing data now with selectable history, cookies/sign-in sessions, cache, website storage/permissions, form data, and open tabs. Bookmarks and downloads are never selected or removed.
- Incognito remains isolated from regular browsing and automatically deletes its temporary profile when it ends.

YouTube fullscreen
------------------
- Reserves the complete lower player-control band for the website.
- QuietShield no longer starts brightness or volume from a touch until the website probe positively confirms that the touch is not on a control.
- This directly addresses slight finger movement on the far-right YouTube cogwheel being converted into a volume gesture and cancelled.
- Visible ten-second controls remain centered and do not own the YouTube control bar.

Activation wording
------------------
- Customer-facing activation pages use Enter your key.
- Administrator, tester, giveaway, and production/test terminology is removed from ordinary activation instructions.
- Internal key-type detection, administrator authorization, family roles, and server licensing remain unchanged.

Retained
--------
- Two Private Browser tabs.
- Premium favicons and long-press Back/Forward history.
- App Connection Lock and parent/child workflows.
- Smart Skip Beta.
- Same-device license restoration.
- PiP remains removed from Private Browser.

Google Play
-----------
This release does not add Google Play Billing purchase-token verification. Existing QuietShield keys continue to use the QuietShield license server. Play Billing requires a separately configured Play Console product and backend verification workflow before it should be enabled in a production Play build.
