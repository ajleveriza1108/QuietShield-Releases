QuietShield 2.5.6 Code 281 Final - Known Limitations
========================================================

- Exact schedule timing requires Android Alarms & reminders access where the
  operating system requires it. OEM battery controls may still delay background
  work.
- If VPN permission is revoked, QuietShield cannot silently restore protection;
  it must notify the user.
- Android can aggregate system or shared-UID traffic. Mobile Data Watch may not
  always assign every byte to one visible application.
- Usage Access is required before QuietShield can read other applications'
  mobile-data statistics.
- Restricting Google or system applications can affect synchronization,
  notifications, downloads, licensing, backup, location, or Play services.
- In strict Block all mode, explicitly allowed user applications route outside
  the strict firewall and do not receive normal QuietShield VPN filtering during
  that strict session. The interface discloses this state.
- Website pull-to-refresh depends on WebView gesture behavior and is intentionally
  disabled on Browser Home and fullscreen video.
- YouTube and other website controls can change independently of QuietShield and
  may require future site-specific maintenance.
- Smart Skip remains Beta.
- PiP remains removed.
