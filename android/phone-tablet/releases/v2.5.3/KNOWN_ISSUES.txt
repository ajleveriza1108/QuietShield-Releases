QuietShield 2.5.3 Code 278 — Known Issues
================================================

1. YouTube fullscreen settings
The YouTube fullscreen settings cogwheel may remain unresponsive on some
layouts even while the adjacent CC control works.

2. Picture-in-Picture
Private Browser PiP remains removed because previous implementations produced a
black video surface on tested devices.

3. Fullscreen video Beta
Brightness, volume, visible ten-second seeking, Lock, and website controls
remain dependent on Android WebView, OEM firmware, and the website player's own
touch behavior.

4. Smart Skip Beta
Smart Skip relies on community timestamps. A skip button may be absent,
incorrect, or delayed. No microphone recording or continuous speech analysis is
performed.

5. Incognito profile compatibility
Safe Incognito isolation requires Android System WebView multi-profile support.
QuietShield refuses to start Incognito when this capability is unavailable so
normal browser logins are not destroyed.

6. Renderer or process termination
A website renderer or the complete app process may still be terminated by the
system, OEM firmware, low memory, or a WebView defect. Renderer recovery reloads
the last known URL but cannot guarantee exact playback position or website
session acceptance.

7. Parent and child workflows
Administrator-parent and child-device behavior must be physically tested on the
intended phone and tablet. Device Administrator and fully managed Device Owner
modes provide different levels of uninstall and installation control.
